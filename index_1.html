Currently

Measuring whether a production line is telling the truth.

Data Scientist Intern at PT Tricada Intronik (Tritronik), Bandung. I built a data quality monitoring framework for a live telecommunications processing pipeline.

The monitor that reported normal while blind

2026 — present

Ordinary status monitoring answers whether the line is running. It does not answer whether what comes off the line is correct. I defined quality across four dimensions, instrumented every stage in Python against a live Kafka broker and PostgreSQL database, and built alerting that flags a developing problem before it becomes a defect.

Then I tested the framework against datasets with faults deliberately injected at known type, magnitude and timing. One of them it could not see at all. When a source stopped producing entirely, there was no data left to inspect, so the monitor reported normal while structurally blind to the stoppage. Reindexing metric computation against a complete expected interval grid fixed it without touching a single threshold.

Precision NTC thermistor instrument

Instrumentation Systems, ITB

A complete signal-conditioning front-end: a Wheatstone bridge feeding a high-CMRR instrumentation amplifier, with Steinhart-Hart linearisation applied to the raw sensor characteristic and multi-point calibration against certified references.

The part that matters is the last step. I produced a GUM-compliant uncertainty budget, so every reading carries a stated confidence interval. A measurement without an uncertainty is an opinion, and it cannot support a pass-fail decision.

Nephro-Sync — wearable biosensor ecosystem

EPIDEMIC 2026, co-authored

A conceptual wearable pairing a passive-sweat electrochemical urea sensor with 50 kHz segmental bioimpedance, for fluid-overload surveillance in pre-dialysis kidney patients. I designed the sensing, control, edge and power layers.

Most of the engineering went nowhere near the headline feature. Flexible electrochemical sensors drift with temperature, so a few degrees of skin microclimate change moves the reading further than the clinical signal does. The dominant error source was the thermometer, not the sensor. A PID-controlled heater holds the microclimate at 37 °C; a pi-filter keeps converter ripple out of the ADC.

How I work

Three habits, and where each one came from.

Go to the process

The two defects I found at Tritronik were both invisible to reviewing the code. They surfaced only because I ran the system against faults I had built myself, where I already knew the correct answer.

Cause, not symptom

A 239% overshoot has an obvious workaround and a real cause, and they lead to different places. Taking the workaround would have quietly undone a pole placement I had designed on purpose.

State the uncertainty

Calibration traceability and an uncertainty budget are not paperwork around the measurement. On anything that drives a decision, they are the measurement.
